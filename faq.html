Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a service?

Browse services, select a professional, choose date & time, and confirm. You'll receive a booking confirmation instantly.

Are professionals verified?

Yes. All professionals undergo background verification and skill assessment before being listed on UstadJi.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Cash, UPI, Razorpay, and major cards. Pay after the service or online as preferred.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. You can cancel from your booking history. Cancellation policies may apply based on timing.

How do I become a professional?

Register via the Professional Sign-up page, complete your profile and KYC, and start receiving bookings.
